Краткое описание
In this paper, we study the gap of the first two eigenvalues of a vibrating string with fixed endpoints as the density function varies over certain class. We exploit the optimality condition and take the direct approach to characterizing the extremal density function. The characterization of the corresponding eigenfunction of the extremizers and the detailed description of the geometric shape are introduced.
