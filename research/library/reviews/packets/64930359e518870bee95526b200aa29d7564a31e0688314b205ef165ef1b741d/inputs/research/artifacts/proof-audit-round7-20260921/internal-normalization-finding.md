# R7-P01: directly referenced FH normalization

The live tools/feynman-hellmann.md uses integral rho*u^2=1 for the ordinary L2 operator H(tau)=-d²+tau*rho and concludes lambda'=integral rho*u². With constant rho=2 and u=sin(pi*x), that normalization gives integral rho*u²=1, whereas the actual eigenvalue is pi²+2*tau, derivative2. The unnormalized correct quotient is integral rho*u² / integral u²=2. This is a direct-source defect of the FH card linked by docs/SL_gap_extremals.tex, not a new defect in the weighted-string spectral theorem. For a differentiable simple generalized eigenpair A(t)u=lambda B(t)u, self-adjoint common forms and <u,Bu>=1 yield lambda'=<u,A'u>-lambda<u,B'u>. The ordinary fixed-L2 and variable-string-weight cases must be separated. This internal finding is an author derivation pending fresh review.
