closure_Hc3 span{pn:n in N}={f in Hc3: 0 notin N implies f(0)=0; 1 notin N implies f'(0)=0; 4 notin N implies f''(0)=0}. Codimension is |{0,1,4} minus N|. Proof: high complete tails with m0>=3 equal compatible polynomials divisible by x^(2m0−2); their closure is ker(f(0),f'(0),f''(0)) by local third-derivative cutoff estimates and a fixed endpoint right inverse. Independent trace vectors are p0,p1,p4, with p4''(0)=−4. This includes sufficiency, not merely necessary traces. Removing p4 gives a codimension-one obstruction; removing p5 alone does not.
