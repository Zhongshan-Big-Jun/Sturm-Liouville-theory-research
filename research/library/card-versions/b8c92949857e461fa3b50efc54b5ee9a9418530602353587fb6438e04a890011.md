## 2026-10-04: A11 arbitrary retained sets in genuine Hc2

At every fixed c>0, use the original complex space Hc2=D(Kc), its genuine graph
inner product, p0=1, p1=x and the original named p_(2m), p_(2m+1), m>=2.
For arbitrary J subset {0,1} and arbitrary Se,So subset {2,3,...}, the selected
family is dense iff J={0,1} and both sum_(m in Se)1/m and sum_(m in So)1/m diverge.
If both sums diverge, its exact closure is {f in Hc2: 0 notin J implies f(0)=0;
1 notin J implies f'(0)=0}. If either sum converges (including finite or empty
retention), the closure has infinite codimension, irrespective of affine retention.

The complete proof is [SL_H2_arbitrary_deletion_proof.md](../docs/SL_H2_arbitrary_deletion_proof.md).
Transport through the real positive Kc is unitary onto L2. The orthogonal equations
at retained m only are values of bounded cubic-normalized analytic Mellin functions.
Half-plane Blaschke uniqueness extends those equations to all m, with m=2 supplied
by its continuous boundary limit, before invoking the established full-tail two-trace
closure. The converse constructs genuine annihilators by the Cauchy Gram distance
product and finite-dimensional quotient, then maps them back through Kc. No recurrence
is assumed on missing indices; density of an enlarged monomial family is not used to
infer density of the difference family. The complex inner-product convention is
explicit. Independent analytic review is separate from all finite scripts.

Closed: the arbitrary-retention Hc2 density criterion and divergent-side exact closure.
