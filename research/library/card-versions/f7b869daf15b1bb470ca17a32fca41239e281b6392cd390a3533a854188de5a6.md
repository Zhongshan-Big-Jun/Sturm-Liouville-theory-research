Set b_n^(r)=J^r e_n, n>=r, r=2 or4. It has zero endpoint derivatives of orders <r, rth derivative e_n, and Legendre support n-r,n-r+2,...,n+r. For s=2 use e0,e1 and b_n^(2). Endpoint affine interpolation leaves a clamped remainder; finite columns through degree N span Pi_N intersect D(Kc). For s=4 use 1,x,E=(3-q6)/16,O=(11x-5q7)/48, q6=x6-5x4+7x2, q7=x7-21x5/5+27x3/5, and b_n^(4). The lifts specify the endpoint values of f and f''; Krein conditions determine f' and f''', leaving a fully clamped remainder. Exact span is Pi_N intersect D(Kc^2). Note03 proves A_(s,c)||w||^2<=||Tw||_(s,c)^2<=B_(s,c)||w||^2 with explicit positive constants independent of N. High Gram offsets satisfy |n-m|<=4 for s=2, <=8 for s=4, with fixed low couplings. Highest-derivative coefficient tails give quantitative approximation; epsilon<A_(s,c) discards no Gram direction. The clamped s=2 high modes are Shen Lemma3.1, credited accordingly; the coupled lifts and graph-norm bounds are this adaptation.
