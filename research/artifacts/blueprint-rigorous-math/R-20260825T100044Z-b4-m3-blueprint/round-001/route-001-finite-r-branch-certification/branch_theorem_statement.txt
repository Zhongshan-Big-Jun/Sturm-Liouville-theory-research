Finite-R seed-branch theorem (route-001, frozen normalization). Let E1, E2,
E5, E6 be the exact residuals in the hash-bound closed four-equation source,
and let u=R^(-1/6)>0, k2=K(u)u, k3=K(u)u+C(u)u^5,
p1=pi/2+A(u)u^2, and p3=pi/4+B(u)u^2. Put
kappa=(18*pi-48/pi)^(1/3). There exist u0>0 and an exact, locally unique,
real-analytic even branch in a neighborhood of the seed for every
0<u<u0 such that E1=E2=E5=E6=0,
K(u)=kappa+O(u^2), B(u)=1/kappa+O(u^2),
C(u)=16/(pi*kappa)+O(u^2), and A(u)=2/kappa+O(u^2).
Consequently K1=C1=0 in the ordinary u expansion,
k3-k2=(16/(pi*kappa))u^5+O(u^7), and the branch exists for every
R>u0^(-6). It has positive symmetric INF block widths, its two half-mode
solutions have respectively one and two global interior zeros and hence are
the lambda_2 and lambda_3 modes, all four switches satisfy the band equations,
the band signs are the INF signs, and the exact finite-u residual Jacobian is
nonzero. There are constants M,u0>0 for which all displayed O(u^2), O(u^5),
and O(u^7) remainders obey the corresponding absolute bounds by M times the
displayed power throughout 0<u<u0.
