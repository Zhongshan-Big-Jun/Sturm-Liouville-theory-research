Finite-interior scale-entry lemma.  Let u range over all sufficiently small
positive reals, set v=u^2, k2=K(u)u, r=k3/k2, p2=K(u)u/2-u^3(p1+p3),
and p2t=r*p2, and evaluate the exact hash-bound residuals E1,E2,E5,E6.
Assume E1=E2=E5=E6=0; K remains in a compact subset of (0,infinity);
p1 tends to pi/2, p3 tends to pi/4, and r tends to 1; and the declared
finite-interior phase denominators remain nonzero.  No regularity or
asymptotic-series hypothesis on the zero selection is assumed.  Then there
are M,u0>0 such that, for every 0<u<u0,

  |p1-pi/2-2v/K| <= M v^2,
  |r-1| <= M v^2,
  |p3-pi/4| <= M v.

Consequently q=(K*(p1-pi/2)-2v)/v^2, Cbr=K*(r-1)/v^2, and
B=(p3-pi/4)/v are bounded.  More precisely every such zero selection has

  K -> kappa=(18*pi-48/pi)^(1/3),
  B -> 1/kappa,
  q -> (18*pi-24-kappa^3)/(6*kappa),
  Cbr -> 16/(pi*kappa),

and eventually enters the two-face analytic-IFT neighborhood used by
INF-R002-FINITE-INTERIOR-SCALE-EXHAUSTIVENESS-V1.  It therefore equals the
unique exact analytic germ in v from the existing finite-branch proof.  The
excluded singular geometries K->0/infinity, r not tending to 1, or vanishing
phase denominators are not classified or refuted by this lemma.
