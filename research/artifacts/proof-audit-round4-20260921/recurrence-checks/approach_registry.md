# Approaches and outcomes

- Factorial second-difference factorization: successful author proof; gives general solution, finite terminal formulas, positivity and both K constants.
- Finite-pole obstruction: successful author proof; no degree bound, includes root-0. Does not assume every root-1 ratio rational.
- Coprime representation classification: successful author proof; detects two cancellation loci and the d!=0 A=0 representation family.
- Historical Laurent diagonal uniqueness: read with original audit, accurately linked and self-contained explanation supplied for root-1 only. The main new proof does not rely on its historical status.
- Finite rational fitting and numerical asymptotic matching: rejected as universal proof mechanisms; historical optional entries retained with explicit scope.
- Homogeneous box closure via rational classification: refuted. Exact root-1 counterexample is in source sec:box and check output.
