## Problem statement (O1', round 2)

Round 1 closed O1' on the diagonal H_beta subclass with finite-degree
polynomial representers (`dense <=> ker(T|B_adm) = {0}`). Round 2 extends the
attack beyond the diagonal:

- Direction A (theoretic): assume the Gram/moment matrix of H is **banded**
  (`<x^i,x^k> = 0` for `|i-k| > m`) and the representer moments
  `a_k^{(j)} = <v_j, x^k>_H` are finitely supported (or banded-compatible).
  Prove that O1' is then decidable by finite linear algebra and give the exact
  decision criterion, or exhibit where the diagonal kernel criterion breaks.
- Direction B (concrete): an explicit non-diagonal H (e.g. weighted L^2) with a
  single non-coordinate representer where the free-base realization is decided
  rigorously (density holds or fails) for every relevant parameter.

Either direction is a valid advance. A full general-O1' resolution is not
expected; an honest RIGOROUS_PARTIAL_RESULT with new STRICT lemmas/proofs or a
decisive example is a success.
