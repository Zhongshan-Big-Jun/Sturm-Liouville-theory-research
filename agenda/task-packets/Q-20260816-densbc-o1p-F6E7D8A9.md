## Problem statement (O1')

Let H be a Hilbert space with polynomials dense (H1) and moments M_k(w)=<w,x^k>_H
(H2).  Let V = ∩_{j=1..r} ker L_j with Riesz representers v_j, and let
Q_sp = {p_n : p_n in V} for the sparse family
p_0=1, p_1=x, p_{2m}=x^{2m} - (m/(m-1))x^{2m-2}, p_{2m+1} likewise (m>=2).
Theorems 1-5 of the O1 run reduce `closure(span Q_sp) = V` to a structured
moment system (kept recursions, runs, free run-base degrees) together with the
O1' decision problem:

> Does some free run-base degree admit a nonzero w in H with
> M_k(w) given by the free assignment + kept recursions, and
> <w,v_j>_H = 0 for all j?

Attack O1' on a structured subclass (diagonal/coordinate; banded/finitely
supported representer moments; or an explicit H, L_j with a rigorously decided
free-base realization). A full general resolution is not expected.
