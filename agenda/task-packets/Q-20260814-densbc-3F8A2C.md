## Project reason for this task

The project has closed the denseness question for the concrete left-definite
spaces H^s[-1,1] (all integer s >= 1) and built general criteria for the sparse
basis {p_n} (docs/SL_denseness_criteria.tex): moment characterization (Theorem 2),
first-moment criterion (Theorem 3, beta < 1), jump-moment criterion (Theorem 5),
diagonal critical exponent 3/2 (Theorem 11).  The summary section 5.5 item 4 keeps
open the general question: a necessary and sufficient condition for polynomial
density in Hilbert spaces constrained by GENERAL boundary conditions.  This task
attacks that general criterion.  A key new phenomenon to exploit (already visible
in the project's diagonal machinery): boundary constraints can RESTORE density -
if V kills the free moment parameters (e.g. V = span{x^2,x^3}^\perp forces
M_2 = M_3 = 0), the sparse family becomes complete for every beta.  The general
criterion must quantify exactly when constraints kill the free parameters.
