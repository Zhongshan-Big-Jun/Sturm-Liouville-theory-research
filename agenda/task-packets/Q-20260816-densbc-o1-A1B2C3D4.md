## Problem statement (O1)

Let H be a Hilbert space containing all polynomials (H1), with moments
M_k(w) = <w, x^k>_H (H2).  Let V = Intersection_{j=1}^r ker L_j be a closed
subspace defined by r independent bounded linear functionals (form (a)).
Let Q_sp = {p_n : p_n in V} be the kept sparse family.  Give an exact,
verifiable criterion for
    closure(span Q_sp) = V
in terms of the interaction between the constraint functionals L_j and the
recursion graph of {p_n}, when the L_j are NOT coordinate projections (so the
moments M_k(w) are not simply forced to zero at a finite index set).

A satisfactory answer should:
1. Express V cap Q_sp^\perp as the solution space of a finite/structured
   linear system in the moment variables M_k.
2. Characterize when that solution space is trivial, in a way that reduces to
   the diagonal Theorem E when L_j are coordinate projections.
3. Identify the first obstruction (which low moments survive) in the
   non-diagonal case.

## Known ambiguities / risks

- The general form (a) may require the Riesz representers v_j of L_j; the
  moments M_k(w) interact with <v_j, x^k>_H.  The criterion should be stated

- It is not known whether the answer is a finite-rank condition or requires
