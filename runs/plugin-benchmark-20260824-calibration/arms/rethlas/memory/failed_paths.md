# Failed / Rejected Paths

- Direct expansion of E C_s^n into a polynomial in cos(y), sin(y) was messy
  and did not yield a clean global proof.
- Counting zeros by trying to locate them in y intervals directly was abandoned
  in favor of the z-variable Chebyshev formulation.
- Pairing roots by symmetry alone was rejected: symmetry gives an even number
  but not the count or simplicity.
- A degree bound alone was rejected: it gives at most 2n, not exactly 2n.
