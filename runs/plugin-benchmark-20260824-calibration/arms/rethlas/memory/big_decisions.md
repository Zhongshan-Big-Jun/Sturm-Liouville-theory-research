# Big Decisions

- Decide to reduce the 2x2 transfer-matrix product to a single Chebyshev
  combination using Cayley-Hamilton for C_s.
- Decide to treat the root-count problem in the z variable rather than in y or x.
- Decide to prove the n roots by alternating signs at the zeros of sin(n*theta).
- Keep the endpoint R=1 as an audit case; the proof extends to all R>=1.
