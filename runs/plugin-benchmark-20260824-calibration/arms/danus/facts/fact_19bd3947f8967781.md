# F6: Q_n is even of exact degree 2n

## STATEMENT
For n>=1 and s>1, Q_n is an even polynomial of degree exactly 2n, with leading coefficient alpha^n.

## PROOF / JUSTIFICATION
P(x)=1-alpha/2+alpha x^2/2 is even, so U_k(P(x)) is even for all k. Hence Q_n is even. The leading term of U_n(P) is 2^n (alpha x^2/2)^n = alpha^n x^{2n}; U_{n-1}(P) has degree 2n-2, so no cancellation of the top degree. Thus degree 2n.
