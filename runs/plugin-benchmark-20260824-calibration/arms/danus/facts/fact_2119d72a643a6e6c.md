# F8: Elliptic representation and monotone argument

## STATEMENT
For x in (delta,1), write P(x)=cos theta with theta in (0,pi). Let r=1/s. Then
Q_n(x)= [sin((n+1)theta)+r sin(n theta)]/sin theta.
Define psi(theta)=arg(e^{i theta}+r), Phi(theta)=n theta+psi(theta). Then
sin((n+1)theta)+r sin(n theta)=|e^{i theta}+r| sin Phi(theta).
Moreover Phi is strictly increasing on (0,pi), Phi(0)=0 and Phi(pi)=(n+1)pi.

## PROOF / JUSTIFICATION
sin((n+1)theta)+r sin(n theta) is the imaginary part of e^{i n theta}(e^{i theta}+r). Since e^{i theta}+r has positive imaginary part on (0,pi), its argument psi lies in (0,pi). Differentiating psi: psi'(theta)=Im((i e^{i theta})/(e^{i theta}+r)) = (1+r cos theta)/|e^{i theta}+r|^2. Since 1+r cos theta >= 1-r >0, psi'>0. Hence Phi'=n+psi'>0. The endpoint values follow from e^{i theta}+r being real positive at theta=0 and real negative at theta=pi, with arguments 0 and pi respectively.
