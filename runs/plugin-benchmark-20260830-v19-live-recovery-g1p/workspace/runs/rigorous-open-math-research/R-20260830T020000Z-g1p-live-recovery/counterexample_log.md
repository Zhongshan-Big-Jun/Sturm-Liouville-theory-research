# Counterexample log

## Abstract scalar witness

The values

```text
a=0, b=1, gamma_1=1, gamma_2=2
```

satisfy the scalar sign constraints isolated by W1 and realize the residual
equality. This is a counterexample to the sufficiency of semiseparability plus
positive diagonal penalties alone.

It is not a Sturm-Liouville branch counterexample and is not evidence that
`KP-DET` fails.
