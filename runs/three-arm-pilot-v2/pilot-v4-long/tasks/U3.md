# U3: Integral invariant cycle theorem for H^1 over Z

Let `X -> B` be a one-parameter family of complex projective varieties over a holomorphic
disk `B`, with the fiber over `0` a simple normal crossing divisor. Let `t in B \ {0}`
and denote the fiber over `t` by `X_t`. Let `T` be the monodromy operator acting on
`H^1(X_t, Z)`. Prove or disprove that the natural restriction map

    H^1(X, Z) -> H^1(X_t, Z)^T

to the `T`-invariant submodule is surjective.

State all external theorems with hypotheses; numerical evidence is not a proof.
