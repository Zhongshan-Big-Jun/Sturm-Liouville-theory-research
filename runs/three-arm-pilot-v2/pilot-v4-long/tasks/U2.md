# U2: Total variation asymptotics for switch-walk-switch on Z_2 wr Z

Let `P_t^x` denote the law at time `t` of the discrete-time switch-walk-switch walk on
`Z_2 wr Z`, where each departure/arrival lamp is resampled independently from
`Bernoulli(1/2)` and the base move is simple symmetric on `Z`.
For `x = (0,0)` and `y = (0,2)`, determine the asymptotic behavior

    ||P_t^x - P_t^y||_{TV} ~ t^{-1/2}

up to positive multiplicative constants.

Prove the asymptotic with explicit constants, state all external facts, and do not use numerical evidence as proof.
