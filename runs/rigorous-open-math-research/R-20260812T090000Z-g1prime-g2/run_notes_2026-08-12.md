## STRICT identities (verified against clean FD at 1e-4..1e-6 level; several
were previously recorded with wrong signs in docstrings / session notes)

1. Geometric convention: moving switch i RIGHT by dx_i converts pat[i+1] ->
   pat[i] on [x_i, x_i+dx_i), so
   delta rho = (pat[i] - pat[i+1]) delta(x - x_i) dx_i = -s_i delta(x - x_i) dx_i.
2. First-order eigenvalue shift: dlambda_k/dx_i = +lambda_k s_i u_k(x_i)^2
   (FD check at a non-critical point: dlambda_2/dx_1 = -48.84090526 =
   +lambda_2 s_1 u_2(x_1)^2, 12 digits).
3. FH jump: dD/dx_i = -s_i f(x_i), f = lambda_n u_n^2 - lambda_{n+1} u_{n+1}^2
   (FD: dD/dx_1 = +21.89540128 = -s_1 f(x_1)).  NOTE: this flips the sign of the
   formula recorded in the session-51 notes (dD/da = -(R-1) f(a) for INF); the
   zero set f = 0 is unchanged, so critical-point arguments survive, but any
   sign-based step must be re-audited under the corrected convention.
4. At band-consistent points: J = D_xF = (D~ + M~)/lambda_{n+1} with
   M~_{ji} = s_i { +2 w_i w_j D/(lambda_n lambda_{n+1})
                   - 2 lambda_n^2 u_n(x_i) u_n(x_j) G~_n(x_i,x_j)
                   + 2 lambda_{n+1}^2 u_{n+1}(x_i) u_{n+1}(x_j) G~_{n+1}(x_i,x_j) },
   G~_k = sum_{l != k} u_l u_l/(lambda_l - lambda_k) (spectral sum; verified vs
   jac_fd to rel ~1e-6 at moderate R; the opposite-sign variant that used pole
   subtraction was wrong, see item 7).
5. Hess(D) restricted to the family = -lambda_{n+1} diag(s) J at critical points
   (FD Hessian with h = 1e-4: max entrywise error 4.6e-3 on entries O(1e3);
   eigenvalues match -lambda (R-1)^2 spec(K)).  The A3 record in the analytic
   file listed the opposite sign; corrected.
6. K := diag(1/s) J is SYMMETRIC because all |s_i| = R-1 (algebraic identity
   from M~ = diag(s) Sym with Sym symmetric; numerically crossK ~ 1e-13 along
   the branches).  Hence det J = (R-1)^(2n) (-1)^n det K, det K = det K+ det K-,
   and (G1') (sgn det J = (-1)^n) is equivalent to det K > 0, i.e. to Hess(D)
   being positive definite at every critical point.
7. regularized_green (pole subtraction at delta = 1e-9) is numerically broken:
   G_mu ~ 4.7e7 near the pole, transfer-matrix relative error ~2.7e-8 leaves
   O(1) absolute errors in G~ (verified against the spectral sum).  Do not use
   it for Jacobian work; analytic_jacobian now uses gtilde_spectral.

## O-3 map: FD-reliable margins along the symmetric branches

Convention: evK are the eigenvalues of K = diag(1/s) J = -Hess/(lambda (R-1)^2)
so INF (Hess positive definite) shows evK < 0 and SUP shows evK > 0.

SUP (pattern [1,R,...,1], D_n max candidate):
- n = 2..4, R in [1.05, 100]: det J sign = (+1)^n always (n=2,4: +; n=3: -);
  all evK+ and evK- strictly positive (Hess negative definite, local max).
- Smallest |evK| at R = 100: n=2: 0.0156 (detJ +345.1), n=3: 0.0185
  (detJ -4.488e4), n=4: 0.0214 (detJ +1.206e7).  Margins decay slowly (approx
  1/R) and stay well above zero on the reachable range.

INF (pattern [R,1,...,R], D_n min candidate):
- n = 2 (R <= 100), n = 3 (R <= 75), n = 4 (R <= 40): det J sign = (-1)^n
  always; all evK+ and evK- strictly negative (Hess positive definite, local
  min, e.g. softest Hessian direction 0.78 at n=3, R=75).
- Smallest |evK| at the largest reachable R: n=2 R=100: 3.0e-4; n=3 R=75:
  2.6e-5; n=4 R=40: ~1e-5.  Margins decay exponentially (det K ~ e^{-c R});
  no uniform bound exists, but no sign change was found.
- The analytic (truncated spectral-sum) Jacobian is UNRELIABLE in the
  near-degenerate regime: at n=3, R=75 it reported detJ = +2.39e-7 with
  |J - J_fd| / |J_fd| = 1.0, while the FD value is detJ = -1.0125e-5 (stable
  for h = 1e-5..1e-7).  The apparent "sign flip" reported mid-session is
  RETRACTED as an artifact of truncation error.

D values and residuals from the scan are solver-based and reliable everywhere
(e.g. n=3 SUP R=100: D = 144.58160; n=4 SUP R=100: D = 224.60256).
