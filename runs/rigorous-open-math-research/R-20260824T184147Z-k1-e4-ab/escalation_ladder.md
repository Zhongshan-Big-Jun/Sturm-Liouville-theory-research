# Escalation Ladder - K(1) strict anchor run

The mathematical routes remained within exact symbolic derivation and
elementary series estimates.  No numerical or background-computation tier was
needed for the strict proof.

The benchmark used two solver configurations:

- Blueprint v2.3 with independent routes and review enabled.
- Bare Codex with plugins, skills, memories, apps, and subagents disabled.

Both arms were allowed live web search.  A local command-helper failure blocked
file-backed commands in both arms.  The failure was recorded as an
infrastructure deviation and did not alter the frozen mathematical payload.
