The source criterion is divergence of sum (lambda_j+1/p)/(1+(lambda_j+1/p)^2); it does not require lambda_j increasing or tending to infinity. The threshold is strict integrability; for p<1 use the quasi-norm interpretation. For g_e=h_e(x^2), ||g_e||^2=int|h_e|^2t^(-1/2); for g_o=x h_o(x^2), ||g_o||^2=int|h_o|^2t^(1/2), with no extra1/2. Multiplication by t^(alpha/2) moves these weighted spaces to unweighted L2. Thus Kc p_(2m) maps to c t^(m-1/4)-A_m t^(m-5/4)+B_m t^(m-9/4), and the odd row to c t^(m+1/4)-A'_m t^(m-3/4)+B'_m t^(m-7/4). Exact coefficients and retained-index moment equations are D1-D4. Arbitrary moment sequences must satisfy the all-polynomial bounded-functional condition D10, not merely separate moment bounds. The monomial superset criterion transfers non-density only.
