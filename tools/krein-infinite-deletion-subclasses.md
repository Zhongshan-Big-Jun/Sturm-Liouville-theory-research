For arithmetic progressions the closure is {f in Hc2: missing p0 implies f(0)=0; missing p1 implies f'(0)=0}. Proof: cut off a zero-trace target near0 in H2, smooth and repair endpoints, then write f=h(x2) or xh(x2), with h'(1)=0. For m=a+d j, b_m'(t)=m(t-1)t^(m-2). Approximate [h'(t)/((t-1)t^(a-2))] composed with u^(1/d) in C1 by polynomials and integrate; the result is a finite sum of b_(a+d j). The endpoint and central conditions are exact. For reciprocal-summable S, the deduplicated shifted exponent superset of D6 has finite Full Muntz sum, hence admits a nonzero L2 orthogonal vector. Invert the parity unitary and genuine Kc to obtain an actual nonzero Hc2 annihilator. Consequently N={0,1} union {4j,4j+1:j>=1} is dense, while N={0,1} union {2^(j+1),2^(j+1)+1:j>=1} is not. Full proofs and the exact unresolved realization bridge are in note04.
