# Research ledger: MIN-REFL-C2-A

## Entry 1: exact dual path geometry

- **Question:** Can `det(H)` be expanded into terms with manifest physical
  sign using only `P>0`, `W>0`, and the odd/even path geometry?
- **Derivation:** Wrote `H=L_tau+diag(z)` with positive path conductances
  and signed ground charges.
- **Result:** Exact all-dimensional rooted-forest formula (report (3.2)).
- **Reliability:** exact proof; symbolic checks are supplementary only.

## Entry 2: coefficient-sign audit

- **Question:** Can Cauchy--Binet give a coefficientwise positive polynomial
  in the negative-edge magnitudes?
- **Derivation:** Expanded `det(G-W)` by diagonal multilinearity.
- **Result:** Every coefficient is a positive principal minor times
  `(-1)^|S|`; strict alternation is unavoidable.
- **Stopping condition:** raw coefficientwise positivity is algebraically
  impossible.

## Entry 3: physical scaling

- **Question:** Can the canonical positive gamma-jump vector expose the
  missing physical quantities?
- **Derivation:** From `v=W^(-1)C gamma` and `f=M gamma`, obtained
  `Hv=-CP^(-1)f`; after congruence, `VHV` is a path Laplacian plus charges
  `q_i=v_i(Hv)_i`.
- **Result:** Exact physical forced-charge forest formula (report (5.7)).
- **First uncontrolled coefficient:** at `n=3`, the connected-tree
  coefficient `Q_[1,2]=q_1+q_2`.

## Entry 4: exact adversarial reduced witness

- **Question:** Do block positivity, alternating event signs, sharp phase
  chambers, and exact `mu=2` phase-fraction forcing sign the first charge?
- **Result:** No.  The exact witness has `q=(-1,-1)`, `e=3/2`, and
  `det(H)=-2`.
- **Scope audit:** It is not physical; the first and middle positive cells
  force unequal values of `(R-1)^2`.  It refutes only the weakened route.
- **Evidence type:** exact rational algebra, nonpropagating obstruction.

## Entry 5: cross-route drift/translation reconciliation

- **Question:** Is an interval charge the C2-D positive-cell drift, endpoint
  defect, or common first-order translation speed?
- **Exact answer:** No direct positive-factor identification is possible.
  Under normalized reflection, `Q_I` scales to `+Q_I/p^2`, whereas the C2-D
  drift and `D` scale to `-D/p^2`, and `D/I` changes sign.
- **First algebraic failure:** `Q_[a,b]` contains the untelescoped interior
  mismatches `v_jR_j-v_(j-1)L_j`; shared contrast does not make their two
  weights or responses equal.
- **Possible bridge:** translation curvature/second variation, not the
  already exhausted first-order translation speed.
