# Chamberwise Jacobian of the two-scalar relay residual

This note identifies the exact local determinant obligation after the full
relay reduction.  It does not assert a sign.

Fix a premise-complete transverse relay chamber and parameters
`p=(mu,q)`.  Let

```text
T_U(p)=T_U^(n)(p), T_V(p)=T_V^(n+1)(p),
A(p)=T_U(p)-T_V(p),
B(p)=I_U(T_U(p),p)-I_V(T_U(p),p).                    (1.1)
```

Let `z=(U,U_t,V,V_t)` and let `Z_mu,Z_q` be the exact hybrid sensitivities.
On cells they solve the ordinary variational equations; at every transverse
switch they receive the saltation update from `hamiltonian_structure.md`.
The explicit `mu` derivative also includes the cell forcing

```text
partial_mu(V_tt)=-2mu rho V.                         (1.2)
```

At an indexed simple zero,

```text
partial_p T_U=-partial_p U(T_U)/U_t(T_U),
partial_p T_V=-partial_p V(T_V)/V_t(T_V).            (1.3)
```

At a common zero `T_U=T_V=L`, the moving endpoint contributes nothing to
the norm-defect derivative because `U(L)=V(L)=0`.  Hence

```text
partial_p B
 =2 integral_0^L rho[U partial_p U-V partial_p V]dt
   + integral_0^L (partial_p rho)[U^2-V^2]dt.         (1.4)
```

Here `partial_p rho` is the distributional moving-interface derivative; the
saltation construction is an equivalent way of accounting for it.  For
switch times `tau_i(p)` and jump convention
`Delta_i=rho(tau_i-)-rho(tau_i+)`,

```text
partial_p rho=sum_i Delta_i partial_p tau_i delta_{tau_i}.             (1.5)
```

Equations (1.3)--(1.5) give an exact `2 x 2` matrix

```text
K_n(mu,q)=D_(mu,q)(A,B).                             (1.6)
```

Local uniqueness on the chamber is equivalent to

```text
det K_n(mu,q) != 0.                                  (1.7)
```

Because Theorem 3.1 is a local analytic change of description at transverse
roots, (1.7) is equivalent to nonsingularity of the switch residual after
the redundant spectral/normalization coordinates have been eliminated by
Schur complement.  The advantage is dimensional: all `2n` interface
variations have been generated by only the two initial-data sensitivities
`Z_mu,Z_q`.  The disadvantage is that a sign for `det K_n` still requires a
global oscillation or twist theorem for the hybrid variational flow.

The exact remaining local route may therefore be stated as:

```text
Prove that the Lagrangian endpoint map of the symplectic relay has a fixed
nonzero oriented intersection number at every premise-complete root, and
prove that the norm-defect row is transverse to the common-zero-time row.
```

Symplecticity alone does not establish either clause.  Grazing chamber
boundaries remain outside the local formula, although Theorem 3.1 proves
that an actual root is transverse and hence lies in at least one such
chamber.
